Frequently asked questions

What does the Free plan include?

Free includes basic maps, SOS and location sharing. SOS and location sharing are free for everyone, in partnership with the mountain rescue service. Offline maps need a paid plan.

What is the difference between Region Pass and Explorer?

Region Pass costs €4.99 per region, per year, for up to 3 regions. Explorer costs €39.99 per user, per year and includes all regions, route planning, GPX export, mountain huts, and wildfire and storm alerts. If you upgrade to Explorer, we credit the price you paid for Region Passes.

What does Group Leader include?

Group Leader costs €89 per group leader, per year. It includes everything in Explorer plus live group tracking for up to 15 people. Only the leader pays; group members use the app for free.

Are there maps outside Croatia, Slovenia and Bosnia and Herzegovina?

No. Ridgeway Maps covers the marked mountain trails of these three countries only. If you also hike abroad, please keep this in mind when you choose a plan.
